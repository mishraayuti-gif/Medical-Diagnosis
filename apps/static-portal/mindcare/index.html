Feelings are signals, not verdicts. Explore a starting point with curiosity and kindness.

Anxiety

A mind and body response to uncertainty, pressure, or perceived threat.

Grounding, steady breathing, and talking to someone can help you feel more anchored.

Depression

A persistent heaviness that can affect energy, interest, sleep, and connection.

Support matters. A clinician can help you understand what is happening and what may help.

Stress

The body’s response when demands feel bigger than the resources available.

Rest, boundaries, and small practical changes can soften the load over time.

Emotional wellbeing

The ongoing practice of noticing, caring for, and communicating your needs.

Wellbeing is not constant happiness. It is having support for the full range of being human.
